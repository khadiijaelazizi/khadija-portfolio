02 — The challenge

One of the hardest SERPs in Canada.

Mode IPTV is a Canadian IPTV subscription service. Queries like “iptv”, “iptv canada” and “best iptv” are fought over by hundreds of providers. The site needed to rank on its brand, on these national queries and on high-intent setup and trial searches.

Huge competitionnational queries with hundreds of competing providers.
High intentvisitors compare plans, trials and setup before buying.
Brand to protectpeople search “modeiptv” directly and must land on the site.

Keyword research: go for the head terms

Instead of hiding in the long tail, the strategy went straight for the national head terms that drive volume.

05 — What happened, and how it’s coming back

A setback, then a comeback.

Around the end of March 2026, changes were made on the site. Weekly clicks fell from 6,864 (8 – 14 March) to 1,385 at the end of April, monthly clicks from 23,425 in March to 8,132 in April (−65%), then to about 3,000 a month from July. Impressions dropped less than clicks: the pages were still visible, but had lost the top positions that bring the clicks.

06 — Learnings & recovery plan

What this project taught me.

SEO review before any changeEvery redesign, URL change or content removal should go through an SEO checklist: redirects, internal links, content and indexation.
One strong pillar, not sixSix near-identical Canada pages brought 82% of the clicks. Merging them into one pillar page reduces cannibalisation and risk.
Head terms can be wonPosition 6 on “iptv canada” and 14K clicks on “iptv” prove the method works on the hardest queries.
Recover with the dataRestore what changed on the top pages, monitor weekly, and rebuild from the queries that still have impressions.
