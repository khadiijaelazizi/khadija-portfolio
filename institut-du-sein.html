A website for a breast surgeon and gynecologist in Tangier: a calm, trustworthy space where patients understand each treatment and can book a consultation in a few clicks.

01 — The brief

Sensitive care, explained with clarity.

Patients who look for a breast surgeon or a gynecologist are often worried. The website had to feel calm and professional, explain every treatment in simple words, show who the doctor is, and make booking a consultation easy, while ranking for “gynécologue à Tanger” and related searches.

ReassureSoft colors, real photos of the practice and a clear doctor profile.
Explain7 treatments presented one by one, each with its own page.
Book“Book an appointment”, phone and WhatsApp always within reach.
02 — Key sections

What I designed, section by section.

institutduseintanger.ma
01

Trust at first glance

Right under the hero, a key-figures bar sums up the practice in four facts, each with a link to go further.

  • 4 diplomas, 1 single doctor, 7 treatments, 5 days a week
  • “See the treatments” and “Book an appointment” shortcuts
  • Overlaps the hero for a soft, premium look
institutduseintanger.ma
02

Every treatment in one place

Seven treatments, from breast cancer to obstetric ultrasound, presented as a numbered list with a preview card that explains each one.

  • Numbered list that highlights the selected treatment
  • Preview card with image, short explanation and link
  • Each treatment leads to its own detailed page
institutduseintanger.ma
03

A doctor, not just a clinic

In healthcare, people choose a person. This section introduces Dr Sefrioui, his specialties and his way of working with patients.

  • Portrait with name, title and specialty badge
  • Short, human text about his approach
  • A quote and a “Discover his background” button
institutduseintanger.ma
04

Gynecological follow-up, step by step

Screening, contraception and menopause are explained in short cards that open on demand, so the page stays light and easy to read.

  • Expandable cards with “Read more”
  • Tags for related topics (colposcopy, pill, IUD…)
  • “Book an appointment” right next to the explanation
03 — Treatment page

Breast cancer in Tangier.

Each treatment has a dedicated page that answers patients’ questions and targets a local search. The breast-cancer page is the most important one.

  • Breadcrumb and a local title: “Cancer du sein à Tanger”
  • An intro that says who takes care of you, from screening to surgery
  • Then a long, structured guide written for patients and for Google
  • Ends with the contact block: phone, WhatsApp and booking

04 — Mobile

Gentle on the phone too.

Many patients search discreetly on their phone. The mobile version keeps the same calm tone and puts contact first.

  • Phone and WhatsApp bar at the very top
  • Large “Request a consultation” button
  • Treatments become an accordion, one open at a time
  • Readable text sizes and generous spacing
