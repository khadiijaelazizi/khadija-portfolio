01 — Starting point

A new store, invisible on Google.

Telju Fitness sells gym and fitness equipment to individuals and professionals across Morocco. When the work started, the site had no organic visibility at all: Google showed it only a handful of times per week, and the very first organic click arrived in the week of 23 November 2025.

0 clicksfrom organic search until late November 2025.
No authoritya new domain, competing with big retailers and marketplaces.
Big catalogue200+ products and categories to structure and optimise.

01

Technical audit & foundations

Before any content, the store had to be crawlable, fast and understood by Google.

  • Indexation checked and cleaned, sitemap submitted
  • Core Web Vitals and mobile-first rendering
  • Structured data (schema) for products and the business
  • Search Console and GA4 set up from day one

02

Keyword research by intent

Keywords were grouped by what the searcher wants, so each family gets the right type of page.

  • Commercial “+ Maroc” queries for categories and products
  • Supplier and gym-owner questions for B2B leads
  • Training and nutrition questions for the blog
  • Brand queries protected on the homepage
03

Architecture & internal linking

A clear WooCommerce silo structure so authority flows from the homepage to categories and products.

  • Homepage → product families → categories → products
  • Breadcrumbs on every category and product page
  • Every article links back to the relevant products
  • 43 category pages and 160 product pages earning impressions
teljufitness.ma/categorie-produit/racks-supports
04

On-page optimisation of 180+ pages

Each category and product page was optimised for the query it should rank for.

  • Homepage H1 built around “Matériel de sport Maroc”
  • Titles and meta descriptions written for click-through
  • Category pages with a clear title, sub-categories and product counts
  • Product names that match how Moroccan buyers search
teljufitness.ma/blog
05

A 90-article content cluster

Guides on training, cardio and nutrition built topical authority and brought most of the site’s impressions.

  • 90 articles with impressions in Search Console
  • Articles = 65% of all impressions (163,756)
  • Top article: 638 clicks and 68,854 impressions
  • Each guide links to the matching equipment

06

Market focus: Morocco, on mobile

The strategy targeted Moroccan buyers first, and the data confirms it.

  • 70% of clicks come from Morocco
  • 74% of clicks come from mobile, which justified the mobile-first work
  • “+ Maroc” and city queries such as “matériel de sport casablanca”
  • French-language guides also reach France, Canada and Belgium

07

Tracking & reporting

Weekly follow-up in Search Console and GA4 to see which pages win and where to act next.

  • Performance tracked by page type and by query
  • Content brings reach, the shop brings the clicks
  • Homepage + categories = 67% of clicks
  • Priorities adjusted every month from the data

05 — Learnings & next steps

What the data taught us.

Content opens the doorArticles bring 65% of impressions, while the homepage and categories bring 67% of clicks. Both are needed.
Lift the CTR of top guidesThe best article has 68,854 impressions but a 0.9% CTR: better titles and snippets are a quick win.
Push treadmills to the top 5“Tapis roulant” queries bring the most clicks but sit around position 9. They are the next priority.
Win back the autumn dipWeekly clicks went from 213 in August to about 130 in October. Fresh content and updates are planned.
