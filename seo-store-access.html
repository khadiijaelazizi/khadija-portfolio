01 — Starting point

From zero authority to a steady audience.

Store Access sells professional IT equipment in Morocco: POS systems, time clocks, access control, smart locks and EV chargers. The store started with zero authority, zero backlinks and an average position of 40. This page shows the following 12 months, when the goal was to grow that visibility and hold it.

02

Keyword research: informational first

Product names alone have little volume, so the plan went after the questions buyers and users ask around these devices.

  • Informational content first, then commercial and price keywords
  • Support questions on printers, cameras and smartwatches
  • “Prix” and “Maroc” modifiers for buying intent
  • EV charging, a fast-growing topic in Morocco
03

Architecture in silos

The store is organised around how Moroccans search: one SEO landing page per product line, then categories and products.

  • 5 SEO landing pages: POS, smart locks, access control, time clocks, printers
  • 29 category pages and 82 product pages with impressions
  • Guides link down to the matching products
  • A silo per product line to concentrate authority
storeaccess.ma/pointeuse-maroc
04

On-page: landing pages that sell

Each product line has a landing page that answers buyers’ questions before showing the products.

  • Titles built on the commercial query, e.g. “pointeuse … Maroc”
  • Intro copy on technologies, delivery time and support
  • Clear call to action, then the product grid
  • Product pages named the way buyers search
storeaccess.ma/comment-installer-une-imprimante-canon-sans-cd
05

A blog of 212 practical guides

Step-by-step guides answer real problems and bring the store in front of a huge audience.

  • 212 articles with impressions in Search Console
  • Guides = 76% of clicks and 89% of impressions
  • Top guide: 2,133 clicks, 136,481 impressions, position 4.7
  • Structured with numbered steps and an FAQ

05 — Learnings & next steps

What the data taught us.

Guides open the doorGuides bring 89% of impressions. They build trust and authority for the whole store.
More queries, lower averageImpressions doubled while the average position moved from 6.4 to about 10: the site now appears on many more, harder queries.
Lift the CTR of big guidesThe smartwatch and smartphone-printing guides have 100K+ impressions each but a CTR under 0.5%. Better titles are a quick win.
Push the money keywords“Serrure intelligente maroc” (27) and “caisse enregistreuse maroc” (22) are still on page 2–3: the next priority for the landing pages.
