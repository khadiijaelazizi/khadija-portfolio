01 — The brief

Many programs, one clear path.

Talensio offers certified trainings for individuals and companies, from HR and audit to Power BI and SAP. The website had to make this catalogue easy to explore, help visitors who don’t know which program fits them, and turn interest into enrolments or a call with an advisor.

Find fastSearch by domain or keyword right from the first screen.
Guide the undecidedA one-click orientation that recommends the right program.
ConvertClear “See the program” and “Enrol” actions on every card.
02 — Key sections

What I designed, section by section.

talensio.ma
01

A hero that is also a search engine

The first screen does more than look good: visitors pick their profile and search the catalogue straight away.

  • Tabs for individuals, companies and custom programs
  • Search by domain and keyword, with an “Orientation in 1 click” shortcut
  • Key figures bar for instant trust (students, years, trainers)
talensio.ma
02

A filterable catalogue of 6 certified tracks

Each program gets the same clear card, so visitors can compare at a glance and act without leaving the page.

  • Filters: Finance & Management, Data & ERP, HR & Operations
  • Short promise, duration and “Certificate” tag on every card
  • Two actions per card: “See the program” and “Enrol”
talensio.ma
03

“What’s your goal?”: guided orientation

Not everyone knows the name of the training they need. Here they choose a situation that looks like theirs and instantly see the recommended program.

  • Goals written in everyday words, not course titles
  • Live recommendation card with what you will learn
  • “See the program” or “Get advice” to keep moving
talensio.ma
04

How the trainings work, in 4 steps

Online training raises questions. This section answers them with a real session preview and a simple timeline.

  • Live session video preview
  • Steps: enrolment, live sessions, practice, certification
  • Reassurance tags: 100% online, personal support, certificate
03 — Program page

SAP Finance & Management Control.

Every program has its own page, built to answer the questions a future student asks before enrolling. The SAP FI/CO Executive Master Class page is a good example.

  • Breadcrumb and “Executive Master Class” badge for context
  • Clear promise: 30 hours of theory and SAP practice (P2P, O2C, R2R, KPIs, month-end closing)
  • Learning outcomes, trainer card and a session video
  • Then methodology, FAQ, a booking form and similar programs

04 — Mobile

Designed for the phone first.

Most visitors discover training programs on their phone. Every section was rebuilt for small screens, not just shrunk.

  • Compact header with a burger menu and quick contact bar
  • Search and audience tabs stacked for the thumb
  • Program cards in full width, filters that wrap neatly
  • Floating WhatsApp button always within reach
